Unit art of Classroom Empires
=============================

The images in this folder (units/*.png) are combinations of open-licensed artwork:

  - Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator
    https://github.com/liberatedpixelcup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator
  - [LPC] Horses, by bluecarrot16
    https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-horses
  - [LPC] Horse Riding, by bigbeargames (based on the horses by bluecarrot16)
    https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-horse-riding-updated-091
  - [LPC] Siege Weapons, by bluecarrot16 and Herodom
    https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-siege-weapons

The full list of works, authors, licenses and links is in credits.json, and each image
lists the works it uses in units.json ("credits"). The game shows these credits on the
"Art credits" screen.

Each combined image is shared under the license written in units.json ("license"),
currently Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC-BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The images were built with tools/art/build.mjs (recolored, combined in layers, cut into
animation frames and packed). The game code is not covered by this file: it is CC0.
